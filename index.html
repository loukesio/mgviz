What to try in the demo

  • Pick a sample or a family in the left panel: every view follows the same focus.
  • Hover a confidence pill in Detections to see the four checks behind it; toggle "Needs checking".
  • Open Taxonomy, switch to the radial figure, drag around the centre to rotate, and choose "Focus on family".
  • Click any virus to open the genome drawer with coverage, ORFs and geNomad; compare it across samples.
  • Export any figure as SVG or PNG, any table as CSV or TSV. Switch the theme in the header.

Both demos are synthetic data generated by mgviz demo; nothing here is a real patient or site.
