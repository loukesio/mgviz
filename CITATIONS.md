# loukesio/mgviz: Citations

## Method

- **EsViritu**: Tisza M. J. *et al.* EsViritu, a tool for the detection and quantification of human and animal viruses in metagenomic sequencing data. https://github.com/cmmr/EsViritu. The mgviz pipeline re-implements its analysis and uses its database (Zenodo [10.5281/zenodo.17716199](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.17716199)).

## Pipeline tools

- **Nextflow**: Di Tommaso P. *et al.* Nextflow enables reproducible computational workflows. *Nature Biotechnology* 35, 316–319 (2017). https://doi.org/10.1038/nbt.3820
- **fastp**: Chen S., Zhou Y., Chen Y., Gu J. fastp: an ultra-fast all-in-one FASTQ preprocessor. *Bioinformatics* 34, i884–i890 (2018). https://doi.org/10.1093/bioinformatics/bty560
- **minimap2**: Li H. Minimap2: pairwise alignment for nucleotide sequences. *Bioinformatics* 34, 3094–3100 (2018). https://doi.org/10.1093/bioinformatics/bty191
- **samtools / htslib**: Danecek P. *et al.* Twelve years of SAMtools and BCFtools. *GigaScience* 10, giab008 (2021). https://doi.org/10.1093/gigascience/giab008
- **pysam**: https://github.com/pysam-developers/pysam
- **pyrodigal-gv**: Larralde M. Pyrodigal: Python bindings and interface to Prodigal, an efficient method for gene prediction in prokaryotes. *JOSS* 7, 4296 (2022). https://doi.org/10.21105/joss.04296; Hyatt D. *et al.* Prodigal. *BMC Bioinformatics* 11, 119 (2010); the `-gv` extension by Antônio Camargo adds giant-virus and alternative genetic-code models.
- **geNomad**: Camargo A. P. *et al.* Identification of mobile genetic elements with geNomad. *Nature Biotechnology* 42, 1303–1312 (2024). https://doi.org/10.1038/s41587-023-01953-y

- **ltc colour palettes**: https://github.com/loukesio/ltc-color-palettes

## Software packaging

- **Bioconda / conda-forge** via micromamba: Grüning B. *et al.* Bioconda: sustainable and comprehensive software distribution for the life sciences. *Nature Methods* 15, 475–476 (2018). https://doi.org/10.1038/s41592-018-0046-7
- **Docker**: Merkel D. Docker: lightweight Linux containers for consistent development and deployment. *Linux Journal* (2014).
